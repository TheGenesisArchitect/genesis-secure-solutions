# Genovus social kit source

Built from `Genovus Logo.pdf` (raster artwork, 3600 × 2025 per page; 7800 × 2025 brand sheet).

- `genovus-avatar.png`: the "Reversed / social avatar" tile from the brand sheet, cropped inside its rounded corners, 1080 × 1080.
- `genovus-app-icon.png`: the "App icon" tile, cropped inside its corners, 1024 × 1024 (Meta app icon size).
- `genovus-facebook-cover.png`: `cover.html` rendered at 1640 × 624 with `render.mjs`. `lockup.png` and `brandline.png` are crops of page 1 of the logo PDF; only the tagline is set in type.

Outputs live in `public/brand/genovus/`. Ask the designer for vector (SVG) artwork before printing anything large.
