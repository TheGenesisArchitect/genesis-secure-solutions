Genovus is a turnkey agency platform operated by Genesis Secure Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when agencies use Genovus and when their customers contact them through it.

## Who this covers

- **Agency clients:** insurance agents and their staff who use Genovus for their websites, social accounts, leads and reports.
- **Their customers and prospects:** people who request a callback, message, or submit a lead form on an agency's site or social pages.
- **People who connect a Meta account:** agency owners who connect a Facebook Page, Instagram account or ad account to Genovus.

## What we collect

| Source | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Agency onboarding | Names, office details, contact details, brand assets, licensed states, approvals | To build and run the agency's site and accounts |
| Callback and lead forms | Name, phone, email, the topic chosen, the consent given, where the person found the agency | To deliver the request to the agency and show where leads come from |
| Connected Meta accounts | Page and Instagram identifiers, posts we publish with approval, comments and messages on those accounts, insights, lead ad submissions | To publish approved content, answer on the agency's behalf, report results and deliver leads |
| Our sites | Basic analytics and error logs | To keep the service working and secure |

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, payment card details or policy documents in our forms, and we ask people not to send them.

## How we use it

To provide the service each agency signs up for; to deliver leads and messages to the agency; to produce the agency's reports; to keep the service secure; and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We do not use customers' information to market our own services to them.

## Meta data

When an agency connects Meta accounts, we access only the assets and permissions the agency grants, through Meta's official APIs. We use that data only to provide Genovus to that agency, under Meta's Platform Terms. An agency can remove our access at any time in its Meta business settings.
