# Genovus data deletion instructions (DRAFT)

> **Draft for counsel review. Not published.** Meta accepts either a deletion callback or an instructions page. Genovus already answers Meta's callback at `/api/meta/data-deletion` with a status link; this page covers everyone else.

To delete data linked to your Facebook account or your information held by Genovus:

1. **If you connected Genovus to Facebook or Instagram,** remove it in your Facebook settings under Apps and websites (or in your business settings under Partners). Meta then sends us a deletion request, and you get a confirmation code with a link to check its status.
2. **Or email us** at [privacy contact email] from the address you used, saying what you want deleted. We confirm when it is done.
3. **If you contacted an insurance agency through a site or page Genovus runs,** you can also ask that agency directly. We act on their instructions for their records.

We complete requests within [period, set by counsel], except where the law requires us to keep something.
